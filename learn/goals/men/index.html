Peptides and men's health

Most results on this page come from small, short studies; the largest body of evidence is for prescription hormone therapy in men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, which produced sperm in 75% of men who had none across 44 studies.10 Studies of peptides for erections and libido are mostly small, and none of those peptides is approved for erectile dysfunction.131415161718 This page maps what people search as peptides for men and what published sources report on each; it makes no promise, and testosterone has its own page.

What "peptides for men" covers

Searches for "peptides for men" mix three different questions: fertility, sexual function, and growth hormone or body composition. Testosterone itself is mapped on Peptides and testosterone. This page covers the rest, plus what the labels of approved weight and diabetes medicines say about men.

Fertility and sperm production

HCG

In a retrospective series of 26 hypogonadal men taking testosterone plus hCG, none became azoospermic (no sperm in semen) and 9 contributed to a pregnancy.2122 The label lists sporadic breast enlargement and occasional fluid retention after high doses,23 and hCG is contraindicated with prostate cancer or other androgen-dependent tumors.24 hCG is a prescription drug, and the prescriber sets the dose.23 Read What is HCG? or see HCG for sale.

HMG

HMG's US label covers only women, but in the UK, Menopur is licensed for men with hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism, given with hCG to start sperm production.2526 Men with primary testicular failure usually do not respond.26 In a 1985 study, adding HMG to hCG normalized sperm counts in 5 of 7 men whose condition began before puberty and who had no history of undescended testes.27 The US label says it should be used only by physicians experienced in infertility treatment, and the prescriber sets the dose.25 Read What is HMG? or see HMG for sale.

Gonadorelin

In a nonrandomized study of 28 men with congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, the 10 on a gonadorelin pump started making sperm sooner than the 18 on hCG plus HMG (median 6 against 14 months).28 Semen usually stays below normal WHO values with either approach.11 WADA bans gonadorelin for male athletes at all times.29 Read What is Gonadorelin? or see Gonadorelin for sale.

Testagen

Fertility claims for Testagen rest on a developer report: partly preserved fertility in irradiated rats, and, in a report on 48 men, sperm counts that rose from 16 to 22 million/mL after a 20-day course of oral capsules.30 None of this has been independently replicated, and PubMed lists no peer-reviewed human study of Testagen.3031 Read What is Testagen? or see Testagen for sale.

Erections and sexual desire

PT-141

PT-141 is bremelanotide, which FDA approved as Vyleesi for premenopausal women with acquired, generalized low sexual desire; the label states it is not indicated for low sexual desire in men, and the prescriber sets the dose.17 The studies in men are small trials, mostly of a nasal form, that showed erectile responses, a 19-man study with sildenafil, and a 2008 trial of 342 men that carries a 2023 expression of concern.32143334 A 2026 journal article still asks whether it should be considered for men.35 The full picture is on PT-141 and men's health. Read What is PT-141? or see PT-141 for sale.

Melanotan 2

In a double-blind crossover study of 20 men with erectile dysfunction, Melanotan 2 led to erections in 17 without sexual stimulation and raised desire, but nausea and yawning were frequent.133637 It is not FDA approved for any use, and priapism has been reported.1638 Read What is Melanotan 2? or see Melanotan 2 for sale.

Kisspeptin-10

In one trial of 32 men with hypoactive sexual desire disorder, a single 75-minute intravenous infusion of kisspeptin-54, a longer form than kisspeptin-10, increased penile tumescence to sexual stimuli and "happiness about sex" compared with placebo, with no side effects reported.1539 It ran at one UK research center.39 Read What is Kisspeptin-10? or see Kisspeptin-10 for sale.

Vilon

In old male rats, Vilon activated sexual function and lowered prolactin; there are no human data on sexual function.40 Read What is Vilon? or see Vilon for sale.

Growth hormone peptides, studied mostly in men

Most human data for these peptides come from men.4142 The sections below show what those studies did and did not measure.

CJC-1295

Of the 63 healthy adults in the three published CJC-1295 studies, all given the DAC version, 87% were men, and two of the three studies enrolled only men aged about 20 to 40.434441 A 2026 review of peptides in sexual medicine found that growth hormone secretagogues reliably raise GH and IGF-1, but evidence for better sexual function, physical performance or quality of life is limited.45 No study reviewed here tested CJC-1295 without DAC for libido or sexual health, and FDA found no pharmacology studies of that form at all.4546 Profiles: CJC-1295 with DAC, CJC-1295 No DAC and CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin. Vials: CJC-1295 with DAC for sale, CJC-1295 No DAC for sale and CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin for sale.

GHRP-6

Most adult human GHRP-6 studies enrolled only men: 17, 18, 9, 9, 9 and 5 healthy men in six separate studies,474849505152 so the adult human data mostly describe men. In them, IV GHRP-6 raised growth hormone, a 34-hour infusion raised IGF-1, and prolactin and cortisol about doubled at the highest single dose tested.474849 Read What is GHRP-6? or see GHRP-6 for sale.

Sermorelin

A retrospective review of 14 younger men already on testosterone found higher IGF-1 after a sermorelin, GHRP-2 and GHRP-6 combination was added.5354 Its trial results in older men are in the questions below. Read What is Sermorelin? or see Sermorelin for sale.

Tesamorelin

Tesamorelin (Egrifta WR) is FDA-approved only to reduce excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV and lipodystrophy, and the prescriber sets the dose.68 In its first phase 3 trial, 86% of participants were men.42 In 13 healthy men, 2 weeks of treatment raised growth hormone and IGF-1 without significantly changing fasting glucose or insulin sensitivity.555657 Read What is Tesamorelin? or see Tesamorelin for sale.

HGH

In a 26-week randomized trial in older men, growth hormone raised lean mass by 3.1 kg;58 the side effects seen in the same trial are in the questions below. Read What is HGH? or see HGH for sale.

Other compounds with data in men

MOTS-c

In 27,527 adults of Japanese descent, men but not women who carried the K14Q variant of MOTS-c had more type 2 diabetes,59 and healthy middle-aged and older men had lower blood MOTS-c than young men.60 These are associations, not results of taking MOTS-c. Read What is MOTS-c? or see MOTS-c for sale.

SS-31

Barth syndrome, the only approved use of SS-31 (as Forzinity, under accelerated approval), mainly affects males, and all 12 patients in its pivotal trial were male.6162 None of the human trials reviewed measured testosterone or sexual function.63 Read What is SS-31? or see SS-31 for sale.

What approved medicine labels say about men

Tirzepatide: Mounjaro and Zepbound

The Mounjaro label reports no effect of tirzepatide on sperm shape, mating or fertility in male rats, and effectiveness did not differ by gender in its trials.64 On Zepbound, hair loss was much less common in men than in women (0.5% against 7.1%).65 Most participants in its sleep apnea trials were men (67% and 72%).66 Read What is Mounjaro?, What is Zepbound? or What is Tirzepatide?.

Semaglutide: Rybelsus

The Rybelsus label reports no clinically significant differences in semaglutide levels by sex, and its effect on blood sugar did not differ by sex; in rats, semaglutide had no effect on male fertility.67 Read What is Rybelsus? or What is Semaglutide?.

How to read this research

Being studied mostly in men is not the same as being shown to help men: the CJC-1295 studies enrolled mostly men but measured only hormone levels and short-term side effects.41 The fertility results come from men with a diagnosed pituitary or hypothalamic condition, not from men with normal hormone levels.1110 Some findings carry a warning flag: the largest PT-141 trial in men has an expression of concern,34 and the Testagen data come only from its developers.30 For approved medicines, the label lists who the drug is for, and the prescriber sets the dose. Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of sermorelin for men?

The studies are small and short. In 10 older men, two weeks at the higher of two doses raised growth hormone and IGF-1 to the levels of young men.12 In an uncontrolled 6-week study of 11 men aged 64 to 76, 2 of 6 strength measures improved, and body composition did not change.34 These trials measured hormones, strength and body composition, not long-term health. The approved sermorelin product, Geref, was discontinued, and FDA withdrew its approval in 2009.5

What are the potential side effects of HGH in men?

In a 26-week randomized trial of older men, 41% of those given growth hormone had joint pain, and carpal tunnel symptoms affected 32% of men given growth hormone plus testosterone.6 Diabetes or glucose intolerance was also more common with growth hormone (18 against 7 men).7 In a review of healthy older adults, breast enlargement (gynecomastia) was more common with growth hormone.8 Somatropin is a prescription drug, and the prescriber sets the dose.9

Can peptides help male fertility?

Hormone treatments can start sperm production in men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, whose pituitary or hypothalamus does not drive the testes: a 2014 meta-analysis of 44 studies in such men with no sperm found that gonadotropin therapy produced sperm in 75%.10 In the congenital form, pulsed GnRH and gonadotropin therapy each start sperm production in about 75% to 80% of men, although semen usually stays below normal values.11 The Endocrine Society recommends against starting testosterone in men planning fertility in the near term.12

Which peptides have been studied for erections?

Three have human studies in men, mostly small. Melanotan 2 led to erections in 17 of 20 men with erectile dysfunction in a crossover study,13 PT-141 gave erectile responses in small trials, mostly of a nasal form,14 and one kisspeptin-54 infusion increased penile tumescence in 32 men with low sexual desire.15 None of them is approved for erectile dysfunction.161718 Sildenafil (Viagra), which is used for erectile dysfunction, works by increasing blood flow to the penis during sexual stimulation.1920
