Finding and ordering a product

How do I find a specific peptide?

Use the search page, or open the shop and pick one of its 12 categories. Every product page lists its strengths, stock status by ship-from origin and the supplies used with that vial. To read about a compound before ordering, open its profile from the A to Z list. Read more: Peptides A to Z.

Is BPC-157 available here?

Yes, as a research peptide sold labeled for research use; BPC-157 for sale lists its strengths (5 mg) and stock by ship-from origin. It is not an approved medicine: FDA's 2026 review found no approved product containing BPC-157 in any country.1 Read its profile for what the research does and does not show. Read more: What is BPC-157?.

Paying for an order

How do I compare vials of different sizes?

Work out the figure per milligram: divide what a strength sells for by the milligrams it holds, then compare across sizes. Check the certificate of analysis too, because the label amount is not all peptide: Bachem, a peptide maker, notes a powder may be only 70 to 90% peptide even when HPLC purity is above 95%.2 Read more: Lab results and certificates of analysis.

Supplies for a vial

What do I need besides the vial?

Each peptide page lists the same five supplies: bacteriostatic water, a 3 mL Luer-Lok syringe, an 18G needle, insulin syringes and alcohol swabs. Bacteriostatic water's label limits it to diluting or dissolving drugs for injection.3 The supplies guide explains what each one is for. Read more: Peptide supplies.

Is bacteriostatic water just sterile water?

No. Bacteriostatic Water for Injection contains 0.9% or 1.1% benzyl alcohol as a preservative and comes in a multiple-dose vial; Sterile Water for Injection has no preservative and comes only in single-dose containers.34 The bacteriostatic water label says it must not be used in newborns, because benzyl alcohol has been linked to toxicity in them.3 Read more: Bacteriostatic Water.

What is the difference between the 3 mL syringe and an insulin syringe?

Markings and needles. The 3 mL Luer-Lok syringe has a barrel marked in milliliters and a threaded tip that a separate needle screws onto.56 An insulin syringe has its own needle and is marked in units: on a U-100 syringe each unit is 0.01 mL.78 For needles, the higher the gauge number, the thinner the needle.8 Read more: Peptide supplies explained.

How are used needles thrown away?

FDA advises putting needles and other sharps into a sharps disposal container right after use, then following local rules when it is about three-quarters full: drop-off sites, household hazardous waste sites, mail-back programs or pick-up.9 If no FDA-cleared container is available, a heavy-duty plastic household container, such as a laundry detergent bottle, can serve instead.10 Never put loose needles in the trash or recycling.10 Read more: How subcutaneous injections are given.

Testing and certificates

Where are the certificates of analysis?

On the lab results page, listed by product and lot when they are published; if your lot is not listed, write to support@d2cpeptides.com with the product, strength and lot number. Check which tests a certificate reports: in its 2026 review, FDA found most certificates for BPC-157 reported purity only, and it treats endotoxin testing as a critical quality check for injectable products.1 Read more: Lab results and certificates of analysis.

What should a certificate of analysis show?

At least identity and purity. EMA's guideline for synthetic peptides lists identification, purity (total and individual impurities), water content, residual solvents, elemental impurities, bacterial endotoxins and microbiological purity among the tests in a peptide's specification.12 Mass spectrometry can confirm identity by molecular mass and sequence.12 Read more: What are research peptides?.

Are peptides bought online always what the label says?

Not always. In a 2024 study of 3 semaglutide vials from illegal online pharmacies, drug content ran 28.56% to 38.69% above the label.13 Health Canada warns that unauthorized peptide products may hold too much, too little or none of the active ingredient, unlisted ingredients, and contaminants such as solvents, heavy metals and particles of fiber, glass or plastic.14 Read more: Peptide safety basics.

What does 'research use only' mean?

It marks a material for laboratory research, not for use in people. Every product here carries this label: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use. The label does not make a product legal to use on people: an August 2026 FDA warning letter found a seller's research-labeled peptides were drugs intended for human use, and Health Canada says such labels do not make products legal.1514 Read more: What are research peptides?.

What a compound is

What is BPC-157?

A lab-made peptide of 15 amino acids, described as part of a protein found in human gastric juice.19 Evidence comes mostly from animal studies: a 2026 systematic review found only three human studies, with 30 people in all and no control groups.20 No country has approved a medicine containing it.1 Read more: What is BPC-157?.

Is tirzepatide a peptide?

Yes. It is a 39-amino-acid peptide joined to a fatty-acid chain, and it activates two gut-hormone receptors, GIP and GLP-1.2122 It is the active ingredient in Mounjaro, approved for type 2 diabetes, and Zepbound, approved for long-term weight reduction and for sleep apnea in adults with obesity.2324 Read more: What is Tirzepatide?.

What is retatrutide?

An investigational peptide that activates three hormone receptors: GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon.25 FDA says it is not a component of any FDA-approved drug, has not been found safe and effective for any condition, and cannot be used in compounding under federal law.11 Read more: What is Retatrutide?.

Is NAD+ a peptide?

No. NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme made of two nucleotides, found widely in nature.26 It carries electrons in energy metabolism and is needed by enzymes such as sirtuins and PARPs.27 The body makes it from tryptophan and recycles it from nicotinamide.27 Read more: What is NAD+?.

Is TB-500 the same as thymosin alpha-1?

No. TB-500 is a synthetic 7-amino-acid piece of thymosin beta-4, amino acids 17 to 23.28 Thymosin alpha-1 (thymalfasin) is a different peptide of 28 amino acids, first isolated from calf thymus in 1977.29 It is approved in some countries, including Italy, but not in the United States.2930 Read more: What is Thymosin Alpha-1?.

Research goals

Do peptides help with healing?

The evidence differs by compound and is mostly from animals. A 2025 systematic review of BPC-157 in orthopaedic sports medicine found 36 studies, of which 35 were preclinical and 1 was clinical.31 Animal results do not show what happens in people, so each compound's profile reports its evidence level. Read more: Peptides and healing and recovery.

Do growth hormone peptides build muscle?

This has not been shown. Human studies of CJC-1295, ipamorelin, GHRP-2 and GHRP-6 measured growth hormone and IGF-1 levels, appetite, diagnosis or gut recovery, not muscle growth.32 Even growth hormone itself raised lean mass by 2.1 kg in a review of healthy young adults without improving strength or exercise capacity.33 Read more: Peptides and muscle growth.

Which medicines are approved for weight loss?

FDA-approved options include GLP-1 drugs: Wegovy (semaglutide) and Zepbound (tirzepatide) are approved, with diet and physical activity, for long-term weight reduction in adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition, and Foundayo (orforglipron) is a daily tablet approved for the same use in April 2026.34243536 All are prescription only, and the prescriber sets the dose. Read more: Peptides and weight loss.

Side effects and safety

What are the side effects of GLP-1 drugs?

Stomach and gut effects are the most common: on the Ozempic label, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain and constipation each affected 5% or more of patients.37 The label also warns of pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, low blood sugar when combined with insulin or a sulfonylurea, and thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.37 Read more: GLP-1 side effects.

Do GLP-1 drugs cause suicidal thoughts?

FDA's review found no increased risk. A meta-analysis of 91 placebo-controlled trials with 107,910 patients showed no rise in suicidal thoughts or behavior, and in January 2026 FDA asked makers to remove that warning from the Saxenda, Wegovy and Zepbound labels.38 FDA still advises anyone with new or worsening depression to tell a health care professional.38 Read more: GLP-1 side effects.

Is BPC-157 safe?

Its safety in people is not established. FDA says compounded BPC-157 may trigger immune reactions and have impurity and characterization problems, and that it lacks enough information to know whether the drug would harm people.39 The US Anti-Doping Agency notes it is unknown whether any dose is safe.40 Read more: What is BPC-157?.

What are the side effects of TB-500?

Unknown in people. In its 2026 review, FDA found no published study in which TB-500 was given to people, and no human exposure data for TB-500 products.2839 FDA also notes it is not a component of any FDA-approved drug.28 WADA bans it at all times as a thymosin beta-4 derivative.41 Read more: TB-500 side effects.

What should I do if I am stuck by someone else's used needle?

FDA says to wash the area right away with soap and water, or use a skin antiseptic such as rubbing alcohol or hand sanitizer, and to get medical attention right away by calling a doctor or a local hospital. The same steps apply if blood or other body fluids reach the eyes, nose, mouth or skin.10 Read more: Peptide safety basics.

Dosage

Do you give dosing advice?

No. Our pages report label schedules for approved medicines, which a prescriber sets for each patient, and the doses used in published studies for other compounds, with species and route. Research-grade vials are unapproved and have no label dose.11 For many research compounds no human dose is established; FDA found too little clinical safety information to characterize BPC-157's safety profile.1 Read more: Peptide dosage basics.

How much tirzepatide do the labels start with?

Both tirzepatide labels start at 2.5 mg once weekly. If more blood sugar control is needed, Mounjaro rises in 2.5 mg steps after at least 4 weeks on each dose, to a maximum of 15 mg weekly for adults.23 Zepbound treats 2.5 mg as a starting dose only, moves to 5 mg after 4 weeks, and lists maintenance doses of 5, 10 or 15 mg for weight reduction.24 The prescriber sets the dose. Read more: Tirzepatide dosage.

Why do GLP-1 labels raise the dose slowly?

To reduce stomach and gut side effects, the labels state.372324 Wegovy injection steps from 0.25 mg to 0.5, 1 and 1.7 mg, four weeks at each; for adult weight reduction, maintenance is 2.4 mg (recommended) or 1.7 mg, and some adults may go up to 7.2 mg.34 Foundayo, a daily tablet, starts at 0.8 mg and rises to 2.5 mg, then 5.5 mg, with at least 30 days at each step.35 The prescriber sets each step. Read more: GLP-1 dosage.

Reconstitution and calculation

How do I work out the concentration after mixing?

Divide the amount in the vial by the volume of diluent added. A 10 mg vial mixed with 2 mL holds 5 mg/mL, so each 0.1 mL holds 0.5 mg, and 0.1 mL is 10 units on a U-100 syringe. The reconstitution calculator and units converter do the same math and show the formula. Read more: Peptide calculator.

How many mL of bacteriostatic water go into a vial?

There is no single volume. The volume chosen sets the concentration, so less liquid gives a stronger solution and more gives a weaker one, and the calculator shows the result for any volume. Approved products fix their own: EGRIFTA WR is mixed with 1.3 mL of bacteriostatic water to give 8 mg per mL.42 Read more: How to reconstitute a peptide vial.

How long does a peptide last after it is mixed?

It depends on the product. Peptide makers advise against storing peptides in solution, because they slowly break down.16 Approved products set their own limits: EGRIFTA SV is used at once, EGRIFTA WR keeps 7 days at room temperature, ZOMACTON 14 or 28 days refrigerated depending on the vial, and one hCG product 60 days refrigerated.17421843 Read more: How to store peptides.

Are syringe units the same as milligrams?

No. A unit mark is a volume: on a U-100 syringe, 1 unit is 0.01 mL, and how many milligrams that holds depends on the concentration.7 FDA has reports of people told to draw 5 units (0.05 mL) who drew 50 units (0.5 mL), ten times the intended amount.44 Read more: Peptide dosage basics.

Comparisons

Is Ozempic the same as semaglutide?

Ozempic is one FDA-approved brand of semaglutide for adults with type 2 diabetes: a weekly injection, also approved to lower heart and kidney risks in some of them, and now a daily tablet too.3745 Wegovy is semaglutide approved for long-term weight reduction, and Rybelsus is a daily semaglutide tablet for type 2 diabetes.3445 Read more: Ozempic vs Semaglutide.

Is Zepbound the same as Mounjaro?

Both contain tirzepatide and are made by Eli Lilly, but they are approved for different uses. Mounjaro is for type 2 diabetes: blood sugar control in adults and children 10 and older and, since August 2026, cutting the risk of major cardiovascular events in high-risk adults.23 Zepbound is for long-term weight reduction in adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition, and for moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity.24 Read more: What is Zepbound?.

Is tirzepatide stronger than semaglutide?

For weight loss, a head-to-head trial favored tirzepatide. In SURMOUNT-5, a 72-week open-label trial in 751 adults with obesity but without diabetes, people on tirzepatide (10 or 15 mg weekly) lost 20.2% of body weight on average, against 13.7% on semaglutide (1.7 or 2.4 mg weekly).46 Waist size fell 18.4 cm versus 13.0 cm.46 Read more: Ozempic vs Tirzepatide.

How do BPC-157 and TB-500 differ?

They are unrelated peptides. BPC-157 has 15 amino acids and is described as part of a gastric juice protein; TB-500 is a 7-amino-acid piece of thymosin beta-4.1928 WADA bans both at all times, BPC-157 in class S0 and TB-500 in class S2.41 FDA's advisory committee reviewed both in July 2026.47 Read more: What is Wolverine Stack (BPC-157 + TB-500)?.

Is sermorelin the same as HGH?

No. Sermorelin is a 29-amino-acid analog of GHRH that prompts the pituitary to release the body's own growth hormone; HGH (somatropin) is the 191-amino-acid hormone itself.4849 In children with growth hormone deficiency, sermorelin given by infusion or in 3 daily doses raised growth rate less than once-daily somatropin at the same microgram dose.48 Somatropin products are FDA approved, while sermorelin, sold as GEREF, was discontinued in 2008.4950 Read more: What is Sermorelin?.
