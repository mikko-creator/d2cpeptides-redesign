Benefits and uses

Kisspeptin-10 benefits and uses

Kisspeptin-10 has no approved medical use.1 In small human studies, given once or infused for up to 22.5 hours, it raised LH rapidly and raised testosterone in men during infusions.23 The better-known fertility and sexual-desire results come from the longer-lasting kisspeptin-54, not kisspeptin-10.45

What kisspeptin-10 itself has shown

Each finding below comes from a small human study in which kisspeptin-10 was given into a vein.

  • Fast LH release (human trial, 6 healthy men, 2011): single IV doses caused a rapid, dose-dependent LH rise that peaked within 45 minutes, though the highest dose gave no significant rise.22726
  • More frequent LH pulses (human trial, 4 healthy men, 2011): a 9-hour infusion raised LH pulse frequency from 0.7 to 1.0 pulses per hour.283
  • Testosterone during infusions (human trials, 2011 and 2013): levels rose from 16.6 to 24.0 nmol/L over 22.5 hours in 4 healthy men.3 In 5 men with type 2 diabetes and low testosterone, they rose from 8.5 to 11.4 nmol/L over 11 hours.6
  • A reset of the brain's hormone clock (human trial, 13 healthy men, 2011): one IV dose reset the timing of the GnRH pulse generator.1529
  • Rare inherited conditions (human trial, 4 patients, 2013): a 12-hour infusion restored pulsing LH release in all 4 patients with neurokinin B pathway defects and raised testosterone in the men.1630
  • Predicting puberty (observational study, 16 children, 2020): all 8 whose LH rose by 0.8 mIU/mL or more after a single IV test dose went on to progress through puberty.17
  • Women (human trial, 35 women, 2011): women in the follicular phase of the cycle showed no significant gonadotropin change, while women in the preovulatory phase had LH and FSH rises after a 10 nmol/kg IV dose.32731

Results from kisspeptin-54, a longer form

Kisspeptin-54 carries the same active end but lasts longer in the blood: its half-life is 27.6 minutes, against about 4 minutes for kisspeptin-10.18 These results come from kisspeptin-54, not kisspeptin-10:

  • IVF egg maturation (human trial, 53 women, 2014): a single injection under the skin matured eggs at every dose tested. Biochemical and clinical pregnancy rates were 40% and 23%, and 10 women gave birth to healthy babies.1920
  • Women at high risk of OHSS (phase 2 trial, 60 women, 2015): eggs matured in 95%, the live birth rate per embryo transfer was 45%, and no woman developed moderate, severe or critical OHSS.425
  • A second dose (phase 2 trial, 62 women, 2017): adding a dose 10 hours after the first raised the share of women with an egg yield of 60% or more from 45% to 71%.21
  • Low sexual desire in men (randomized trial, 32 men, 2023): one 75-minute infusion increased penile tumescence in response to sexual stimuli and 'happiness about sex' compared with placebo, at a single UK research center.58
  • Low sexual desire in women (randomized trial, 32 women, 2022): an infusion changed sexual and facial attraction processing in the brain, and the authors called for larger trials.1233
  • Mood (human study, 29 healthy young men, 2017): an infusion reduced negative mood and increased limbic brain responses to sexual and couple-bonding images.13
  • Insulin (human study, 15 healthy men, 2018): an infusion increased insulin release after an IV glucose load.22

Research on synthetic kisspeptin analogs

Longer-acting synthetic molecules that act on the same receptor have also been studied, two of them for the opposite effect: suppressing testosterone. Given continuously, TAK-683 suppressed testosterone below castration level in 4 of 5 men by day 7 at the top dose (human trial, 60 healthy men).934 TAK-448 did the same by day 8 in healthy men and was also tested in men with prostate cancer (two phase 1 studies).10 As MVT-602, TAK-448 produced an LH rise similar in size to kisspeptin-54 but much later, in 24 women (human study, 2020).2335

What has not been shown

  • Fertility outcomes with kisspeptin-10: its studies measured hormone levels, not pregnancies or sperm counts.37
  • Lasting effects: no kisspeptin-10 study reviewed gave it for longer than 22.5 hours, so there are no data on weeks of use.3
  • Weight loss: not shown. In 15 healthy men, kisspeptin-54 did not change gut hormones, appetite or food intake.22
  • Memory or thinking: not tested in the studies reviewed. The only related data are the mood and brain-imaging results with kisspeptin-54.13
  • Insulin with kisspeptin-10: a registered study in 14 men finished in January 2014 and has posted no results.24

Frequently asked questions

Does kisspeptin raise testosterone levels?

In small studies of men, yes, during infusions. A 22.5-hour IV kisspeptin-10 infusion raised testosterone from 16.6 to 24.0 nmol/L in 4 healthy men.3 An 11-hour infusion raised it from 8.5 to 11.4 nmol/L in 5 men with type 2 diabetes and low testosterone.6 Single IV doses gave only small rises,7 and a 75-minute kisspeptin-54 infusion did not change testosterone.8

Is kisspeptin good for testosterone?

A lasting benefit has not been shown, and steady exposure has had the opposite effect in some studies. The kisspeptin-10 testosterone studies lasted hours, not weeks.36 In healthy men, continuous dosing with long-acting kisspeptin analogs at the higher doses tested drove testosterone into the castration range by day 7 to 8,910 and continuous high-dose kisspeptin-10 suppressed LH pulses in monkeys.11 Other compounds are compared on peptides and testosterone.

How long does it take to feel kisspeptin?

The studies reviewed here did not describe a distinct feeling. What researchers measured was hormonal: LH rose rapidly after an IV kisspeptin-10 dose, peaking within 45 minutes in healthy men.226 Trials of 75-minute kisspeptin-54 infusions in 32 men and 32 women reported no side effects,812 and a study of 29 healthy young men found less negative mood alongside changes on brain scans.13

Can kisspeptin help with fertility?

The fertility evidence comes from IVF and used kisspeptin-54. One injection led to mature eggs in 95% of 60 women at high risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS), with a 45% live birth rate per embryo transfer and no moderate or severe OHSS.425 In 2015, the researchers said larger randomized trials against the triggers then in use were warranted.14
