Insulin syringes 1 mL syringes with their own fine needle, marked in units5 1 unit equals 0.01 mL on a U-100 scale3

Frequently asked questions

Which supplies does a peptide vial need?

Every peptide page lists the same five: bacteriostatic water to dissolve the powder, insulin syringes and a 3 mL Luer-Lok syringe for measuring, an 18G needle for drawing up and mixing, and alcohol swabs for the vial stopper. The reconstitution guide puts them in order.

What is the difference between the 18G, 23G and 25G needles?

A bigger gauge number means a thinner needle, so 18G is the widest of the three and 25G the thinnest.5 All three are 1 inch long. The 23G and 25G are BD PrecisionGlide needles, and the supplier lists the 18G for drawing up and mixing rather than injecting. Each attaches to a syringe by its hub, for example by screwing onto the threaded tip of the 3 mL Luer-Lok syringe.4

Can I order supplies in bulk?

Yes. A wholesale account covers both peptides and supplies, and wholesale terms are confirmed on application through the wholesale application. Single items, such as one needle or one vial of bacteriostatic water, can also be ordered from each supply's own page, in the same cart as peptide vials.
