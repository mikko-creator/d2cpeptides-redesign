Research use and the law

Every product here is labeled: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use. That label has legal limits. Federal rules let an unapproved new drug be shipped for test-tube or lab animal research only under a caution that ends "Not for use in humans," and the shipper must check that the buyer regularly does such research.6 Under federal rules, a product's intended use can be shown by its labeling claims, advertising, the seller's statements and the circumstances of its sale.7 In warning letters dated August 24, 2026, FDA told three online peptide sellers that research-use labels did not change its finding: their websites showed the products were meant as drugs for human use.8910 Read our research use policy before you order.

How to check quality

A certificate of analysis should do more than print a purity figure. FDA's Q7 guidance for makers of active drug ingredients says certificates should be issued for each batch on request, list each test with its acceptance limits and numerical results, be dated and signed by the quality unit, and give the original manufacturer's name, address and telephone number.11 EMA's synthetic peptide guideline lists tests a peptide's specification may include, among them identity, total and individual impurities, water content, residual solvents, elemental impurities, bacterial endotoxins and microbiological purity.12 For a peptide medicine, the European Pharmacopoeia thresholds that EMA cites ask for peptide-related impurities to be reported above 0.1%, identified above 0.5% and qualified above 1.0%.12 Hold any vendor's documents up against that list.

Do you ship peptides from Canada?

Orders ship from the USA and Canada, and each product page shows where each of its strengths can ship from. Delivery goes to addresses across the United States; see the shipping page for details. If you want to buy peptides in Canada for a Canadian address, note that Health Canada said in April 2026 that a research-use label does not make a peptide product legal or exempt from its rules, and it advised Canadians not to buy or use such products.1

How do I choose the best peptide vendor?

Ask each peptide vendor for a certificate of analysis for the lot you are buying, with a numerical result for each test rather than a pass mark. FDA's Q7 guidance expects a certificate to give the batch number and numerical test results.11 Testing of products bought online shows why. A 2018 Belgian study of falsified peptide drugs from suspected illegal internet pharmacies found purity of 5% to 75% for cysteine-containing peptides, plus arsenic and lead.2 A 2024 lab analysis of 3 semaglutide vials from illegal online pharmacies measured purity of 7.7% to 14.37%, against 99% claimed on the labels.3 The lab results page lists our documents by product and lot as they become available.

What supplies do I need with a peptide vial?

A freeze-dried (lyophilized) peptide needs a sterile diluent before use. FDA's inspection guide on freeze-drying lists the better stability of a dry powder among the process's advantages, and the need for a sterile diluent at reconstitution among its drawbacks.4 Bacteriostatic Water for Injection, USP is sterile water for injection with 0.9% or 1.1% benzyl alcohol added as a preservative.5 Diluent, syringes, needles and swabs are on the supplies page, and the reconstitution calculator works out the concentration for a chosen volume.
