Frequently asked questions

Why can't you buy BPC 157 anymore?

BPC-157 is still sold here, labeled for research use only. The restrictions are on pharmacy compounding. FDA placed BPC-157 in Category 2, its group for significant safety concerns, and after the nominators withdrew it FDA still lists possible immune reactions, impurity problems and too little safety information to know whether it would harm people.2 An FDA advisory committee narrowly backed it for compounding in July 2026, but FDA must still accept that nonbinding vote and go through rulemaking.3

Where can I buy TB500 peptide?

TB-500 is in this category, and the TB-500 product page lists its strengths, price and stock. Before planning work with it, note what regulators have recorded. FDA says it has not identified any human exposure data for TB-500, which it lists as a fragment of thymosin beta-4 (LKKTETQ).2 WADA's 2026 list bans thymosin beta-4 derivatives such as TB-500 at all times.4 TB-500 is also sold with BPC-157 in one vial as the Wolverine Stack.

Is BPC-157 approved by the FDA for human use?

No. The US Anti-Doping Agency says BPC-157 is not approved for human clinical use by FDA or by any other health authority in the world.5 WADA's 2026 list bans it at all times as a non-approved substance.4 When FDA's advisory committee reviewed BPC-157 in July 2026, the use on the table was ulcerative colitis, not injury repair.1 The BPC-157 education page reviews the published studies.
