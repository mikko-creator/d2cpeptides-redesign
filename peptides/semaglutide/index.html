About Semaglutide

Semaglutide is a peptide of 31 amino acids and a GLP-1 receptor agonist, meaning it activates the receptor for the hormone GLP-1.1 Its 2015 discovery paper reports two amino acid swaps from human GLP-1 (Aib at position 8, Arg at position 34) and a fatty acid side chain at lysine 26 that raises albumin binding and prolongs its action.2

The human results below come from randomized, placebo-controlled trials of semaglutide, not of any research-labeled vial.9101213

Research highlights

  • Animal study (2015): in mini-pigs, plasma half-life was 46.1 hours after an intravenous dose, and mean residence time was 63.6 hours after a dose under the skin.2
  • Human trial, STEP 1 (2021): in 1,961 adults without diabetes who had obesity, or overweight plus a weight-related condition, average weight change over 68 weeks was -14.9% with semaglutide vs -2.4% with placebo, both alongside lifestyle intervention.10
  • Human trial extension (2022): of 327 STEP 1 participants followed for a year after treatment stopped, those who had taken semaglutide regained two-thirds of the weight they had lost.11
  • Human trial, SELECT (2023): in 17,604 adults aged 45 or older with cardiovascular disease and overweight or obesity but no diabetes, cardiovascular death, nonfatal heart attack or nonfatal stroke occurred in 6.5% on semaglutide vs 8.0% on placebo over a mean follow-up of 39.8 months. Adverse events led 16.6% vs 8.2% to stop the trial drug.9
  • Human trial, FLOW (2024): in 3,533 people with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease, the risk of the main outcome (kidney failure, a drop of at least 50% in kidney filtration rate, or death from kidney or cardiovascular causes) was 24% lower than with placebo.12
  • Human trial, ESSENCE (2025): in a planned interim analysis of the first 800 people with the liver disease MASH and fibrosis stage 2 or 3, steatohepatitis resolved without worse fibrosis in 62.9% on semaglutide vs 34.3% on placebo at 72 weeks.13

Regulatory status

Research-labeled semaglutide is not FDA approved, and no approved application covers it.614 FDA sent warning letters to online sellers of semaglutide labeled for research use in February 2024, December 2024 and February 2025.15614 In each, FDA said the seller's website showed the products were intended as drugs for human use despite the research-only labels, which made them unapproved new drugs.15614 In sport, semaglutide is not prohibited: it sits in WADA's Monitoring Program, which covers substances not on the Prohibited List, and WADA kept the status of GLP-1 receptor agonists unchanged for 2027.1617
