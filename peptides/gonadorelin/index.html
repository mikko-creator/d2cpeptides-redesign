About Gonadorelin

DrugBank describes gonadorelin as GnRH made by solid-phase peptide synthesis, and states that it mainly stimulates the making and release of LH.13 Its initial half-life is 2 to 10 minutes, which DrugBank says requires infusion pumps for clinical use.3 Kisspeptin acts one step earlier in the same chain, by stimulating GnRH-driven hormone release, and is listed as Kisspeptin-10 in the Reproductive Health Peptides range.16

Gonadorelin for sale here is listed as a 2 mg vial of the acetate salt; the gonadorelin price and stock at each shipping origin are shown in the selector above. The clinical record and safety reports are on What is Gonadorelin?, worth reading before you buy gonadorelin for a study.

Research highlights

  • Ovulation, 1,002 women (meta-analysis of 35 studies, 2018): pulsed GnRH appeared to give high ovulation rates in hypothalamic amenorrhea, with similar rates under the skin and into a vein.17
  • Pregnancy against gonadotropins, 30 women (randomized trial, 2013): with hypothalamic amenorrhea plus PCOS, clinical pregnancy was 46% with pulsatile GnRH and 0% with FSH plus LH.18
  • Sperm production, 28 men with congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (nonrandomized study, 2019): sperm appeared sooner with a gonadorelin pump than with cyclical gonadotropins (median 6 against 14 months); over 24 months, 90% of 10 and 83.3% of 18 men produced sperm, not a significant difference.19 Testosterone rose and stayed in the normal range in both groups.20
  • Bone density, 22 young men (before-and-after study, 2015): spine and hip bone mineral density increased after 6 months of pulsatile gonadorelin in hypogonadotropic hypogonadism.21
  • Pituitary testing (records review, 234 girls, 2018): one IV dose of gonadorelin acetate served as a stimulation test in girls checked for early puberty, where a peak LH above 5.0 U/L counted as diagnostic.22
  • Delivery pattern (animal study, rhesus monkeys with hypothalamic lesions, 1978): hourly GnRH pulses brought back pituitary hormone release, but a steady infusion could not sustain it.23

Regulatory status

No FDA-approved gonadorelin product for human use is marketed in the US: Drugs@FDA lists Factrel (gonadorelin hydrochloride, approved 1982) and Lutrepulse Kit (gonadorelin acetate, approved 1989) as discontinued.4 FDA-approved gonadorelin animal drugs are cattle injectables that only a licensed veterinarian may use or order.5 FDA's 503B list places gonadorelin acetate in category 1, under evaluation, and gonadorelin is not on FDA's list of compounding substances that may present significant safety risks.2425 In a June 17, 2026 warning letter, FDA named a "Gonadorelin" sold online by Wholesale Peptide with "RESEARCH USE ONLY" labels as an unapproved new drug.27 WADA's 2026 Prohibited List bans it at all times for male athletes, under S2.2.1.26

Handling and storage

In a 1990 lab study, gonadorelin in water was most stable near pH 5.0.15 Acetate buffer favored stability, while phosphate buffer sped breakdown; a buffered formula (3% mannitol, 0.1 M acetate, pH 5.0) was predicted to lose 10% in 9.0 years at 20 °C.15 GenScript notes that peptides have a much shorter shelf life in solution than freeze-dried.28 For mixing, the reconstitution guide sets out sterile lab steps, and the reconstitution calculator turns the vial amount and diluent volume into a concentration.

How should Gonadorelin be stored when it arrives?

The listing gives no storage temperature; GenScript, a peptide maker, advises -20 °C away from bright light for freeze-dried peptides.28 It lists tryptophan, which gonadorelin contains, as prone to oxidation, and histidine and arginine, also in its sequence, as prone to taking up moisture, so vials stay tightly capped.288 The refrigerated FDA-approved gonadorelin solutions are cattle drugs, not research vials.1314 More in how to store peptides.

What strength is the Gonadorelin vial?
