About Tesamorelin

Tesamorelin is the full 44-amino-acid sequence of human growth hormone-releasing factor (GRF, also called GHRH) with one addition: a hexenoyl group, a 6-carbon chain with one double bond, fixed to the tyrosine at the start.12 In lab (in vitro) tests it binds and activates human GRF receptors about as potently as the body's own hormone.1 GRF acts on the growth hormone-producing cells of the pituitary, which make and release the body's own growth hormone in pulses; with tesamorelin, IGF-1 rises in turn.1

It is absorbed poorly and cleared fast: under-the-skin bioavailability of the original formulation was under 4%, and the mean half-life in healthy adults is about 8 minutes (Egrifta SV) to 11 minutes (Egrifta WR).17 Sermorelin is a shorter relative that stops at the first 29 amino acids of GHRH.8

The tesamorelin for sale here is a research vial, listed with the other growth hormone peptides. For side effects, trials and regulatory history, read What is Tesamorelin? before you buy tesamorelin online for a study.

Research highlights

  • Randomized trial, 412 people with HIV and abdominal fat (26 weeks): visceral fat fell 15.2% against a 5.0% rise on placebo, and triglycerides fell 50 mg/dL against a 9 mg/dL rise.9
  • Pooled analysis of two phase 3 trials, 806 people with HIV: at 26 weeks visceral fat fell, a treatment effect of -15.4% against placebo, with no significant change in fat under the skin and no clinically meaningful glucose differences.10
  • After stopping (52-week extensions): visceral fat built up again once tesamorelin was stopped, and the group switched to placebo lost 1.7 to 1.8 kg of lean body mass between weeks 26 and 52.111
  • Liver fat, 60 people with HIV and fatty liver disease (12 months): liver fat fraction fell 4.1 points more than on placebo, a 37% relative reduction from baseline.12
  • 60 people with abdominal obesity and reduced growth hormone secretion (randomized, 12 months): visceral fat fell 16 cm2 while it rose 19 cm2 on placebo, and triglycerides and C-reactive protein improved.13
  • 13 healthy men, no placebo group (2 weeks): basal and pulsed growth hormone rose and IGF-1 climbed by an average of 181 µg/L, with no significant change in fasting glucose.14

Regulatory status

FDA first approved tesamorelin, as Egrifta from Theratechnologies, on November 10, 2010.151 It is a prescription injection with one approved use, and its label says it is not indicated for weight loss.1 FDA regulates it as a biologic under BLA 022505, and FDA's 2020 notice says biologics that require a license cannot use the 503A or 503B compounding exemptions.1516

In warning letters dated August 24, 2026, FDA called research-labeled tesamorelin products from two online peptide sellers unapproved new drugs.1718 It said research-use labeling did not change that, because the websites showed the products were meant for people.1718 WADA's 2026 Prohibited List names tesamorelin under S2.2.4, growth hormone releasing factors, banned at all times in and out of competition.19

Handling and storage

On the approved labels, Egrifta SV is rolled and Egrifta WR swirled after the diluent goes in, never shaken, and the Egrifta WR solution must be clear and colorless.71 Those steps belong to the Egrifta formulations, with their own inactive ingredients, not to a research vial.1
