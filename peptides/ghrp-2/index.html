About GHRP-2

GHRP-2 (pralmorelin) is built from six amino acids, one of them D-2-naphthylalanine, which the body does not produce.3 By activating the ghrelin receptor, it releases growth hormone through actions at both the hypothalamus and the pituitary gland.1920 It began as one of a set of peptides from Polygen (Germany) and Tulane University (US).8 Kaken Pharmaceutical of Japan later acquired worldwide rights and developed it as KP-102.820

GHRP-2 for sale here comes as a 5 mg research vial, and the selector shows the GHRP-2 price and stock for each shipping origin. Before you buy GHRP-2 online for a study, read What is GHRP-2? for side effects, study doses and the full regulatory record.

Research highlights

  • Diagnosis, Japan (2007, 135 people): one IV dose produced mean growth hormone peaks of 84.6 µg/L in 77 healthy volunteers versus 1.36 µg/L in 58 adults with severe deficiency.9
  • Post-marketing survey, Japan (2006 to 2012, 502 patients): against diagnosis with two or more other tests, sensitivity was 0.853 and specificity 0.885 for severe growth hormone deficiency.6
  • Appetite (2005, 7 lean men): a 270-minute infusion under the skin raised buffet intake 35.9% over saline, and all 7 men ate more.10
  • Older adults (2004, 17 men and women): 30 days of continuous infusion under the skin kept pulsatile growth hormone and IGF-1 elevated.11
  • Pigs (1998): GHRP-2 was more potent than GHRP-6 or ipamorelin yet gave a lower peak growth hormone level, and unlike ipamorelin it raised ACTH and cortisol.12
  • Young adults (1997, 6 people): one IV dose released more growth hormone than GHRH and raised prolactin, ACTH and cortisol, the latter two about as much as the hormone CRH.13
  • Rats (2025): after a rotator cuff tear, GHRP-2 improved healing scores and bone density at 8 weeks and made the repair biomechanically stronger.14

Regulatory status

GHRP-2 has no FDA approval; openFDA's Drugs@FDA data returned no application for pralmorelin on October 24, 2026.15 Japan approved GHRP KAKEN 100 for Injection on October 22, 2004, as a prescription test for growth hormone deficiency.65 Japan's regulator reported in 2014 that no other country had approved or sold the product.6 Since September 29, 2023, FDA has placed GHRP-2 for injectable and nasal use in category 2 for outsourcing facilities: bulk substances that may present significant safety risks.16 FDA cited a possible risk of immune reactions and reports of serious adverse events whose cause was not established.16 WADA bans it at all times under class S2.2.4 on the 2026 List and on the 2027 List, which takes effect January 1, 2027.1718 Every vial is labeled: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.
