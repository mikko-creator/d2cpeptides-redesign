About Kisspeptin-10

Kisspeptin-10 is the last 10 amino acids of kisspeptin-54 (also called metastin): positions 112 to 121 of the human KISS1 precursor in UniProt, ending in phenylalanine amide.2 In rodent and monkey studies, kisspeptin acted directly on GnRH-releasing nerve cells in the hypothalamus through its receptor. That GnRH makes the pituitary release LH and FSH, the hormones that drive the testes and ovaries.3 GnRH itself is listed here as gonadorelin, in the Reproductive Health Peptides range.

This page lists kisspeptin for sale as a 5 mg research vial; the options above show the kisspeptin price, stock and shipping origins. For the mechanism, side effects and full source list, read What is Kisspeptin-10? before you buy kisspeptin online for a study.

Research highlights

  • Fast LH rise, 6 healthy men (2011): single IV doses raised LH rapidly, peaking within 45 minutes; the rise was significant at 4 of the 6 doses tested, but not at the highest.162425
  • Testosterone during infusion, 4 healthy men (2011): over a 22.5-hour IV infusion, testosterone climbed from 16.6 to 24.0 nmol/L.4
  • Sex differences, 11 men and 35 women (2011): IV doses raised LH in men, follicular-phase women responded much less, and injections under the skin did not significantly change their hormones.52627
  • Inherited NKB-pathway defects, 4 patients (2013): during a 12-hour IV infusion, all 4 regained pulsatile LH secretion, with no adverse events related to kisspeptin-10.17
  • Delayed puberty, 16 children (observational, 2020): all 8 whose LH rose by 0.8 mIU/mL or more after one IV test dose later progressed through puberty.1813
  • Head-to-head, healthy men (2015): in 3-hour IV infusions, kisspeptin-10 and kisspeptin-54 had similar effects, while GnRH raised LH and FSH about 3 times more than kisspeptin-10.282930

Regulatory status

Kisspeptin-10 is not FDA approved, and a DailyMed search returns no kisspeptin drug labels.6 On September 29, 2023, FDA put it on its list of compounding substances that may pose significant safety risks, naming possible immune reactions, peptide impurities and limited or no safety data.1431 It remains in 503A Category 2 on FDA's list updated May 14, 2026.11 The WADA 2026 Prohibited List names kisspeptin and its agonist analogues under S2.2.1, banned for male athletes in and out of competition.15 The listing carries this notice: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.

Handling and storage

Trial teams reconstituted freeze-dried kisspeptin-10 in sterile 0.9% saline: 0.5 ml per vial in the 2015 UK study, and 1 mg in 5 ml in a 2011 study.1023 For IV infusions, it was dissolved in saline containing 5% gelofusine.32 Its sequence includes asparagine and tryptophan, which Bachem links to a limited shelf life, and GenScript lists tryptophan as prone to oxidation and arginine as prone to absorbing moisture.7192021 Lab steps are in the reconstitution guide, and the reconstitution calculator gives the concentration for a chosen diluent volume.

There is no drug label with official storage directions for kisspeptin.6 For freeze-dried peptides in general, Bachem, a peptide maker, says room-temperature shipping and short-term refrigeration at 4 °C are acceptable, and advises long-term storage below -15 °C.19 Bachem also advises warming a cold vial to room temperature before opening it, because peptides tend to absorb moisture.1920 More in how to store peptides.
