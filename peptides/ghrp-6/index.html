About GHRP-6

GHRP-6 is a lab-made peptide of six amino acids, His-D-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2, with D-forms of tryptophan and phenylalanine at positions 2 and 5.21 The first report, in 1984, found it released growth hormone from incubated pituitary tissue and in live rats, monkeys, lambs and calves, with no rise in LH, FSH, TSH or prolactin.4 It predates the discovery of its target: the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) was cloned in 1996, and its natural partner, the stomach hormone ghrelin, was identified in 1999.56 A second binding site, CD36, has been tied to cell-survival signaling in cell and animal work.7

Researchers looking for GHRP-6 for sale will find it here as a single 5 mg vial, and the selector above shows the GHRP-6 price and stock at each shipping origin. Side effects, study doses and the full source list are in What is GHRP-6?, worth reading before you buy GHRP-6 online for a project.

Research highlights

  • Healthy men (1989, 17 men): 30-minute IV infusions reliably raised growth hormone above a threshold dose, did not move LH, FSH, TSH or ACTH, and were well tolerated.8
  • Synergy (1990, 18 men): low IV doses acted synergistically with GHRH; prolactin and cortisol about doubled, but only at the highest dose tested.9
  • Dependence on GHRH (1998, 9 men): blocking the body's own GHRH cut the peak growth hormone rise from 33.8 to 6.2 µg/L.10
  • 34-hour infusion (1993, 9 young men): integrated growth hormone roughly doubled against saline and IGF-1 climbed from 252 to 312 µg/L, with no loss of pituitary response.11
  • Diagnosis (2000, 250 adults): given with GHRH as a single IV test, it separated adults with growth hormone deficiency from healthy adults at a 15 µg/L cut-off, and the hormone peaks caused no side effects.12
  • Sleep (1995, placebo-controlled, healthy men): four night-time IV doses lengthened stage 2 sleep (270 vs 245 minutes) without changing deep sleep, while raising ACTH and cortisol.13
  • Pigs (2007): in a heart attack model, damaged heart tissue weighed 78% less and the infarct was 50% thinner than with saline.14
  • Evidence level (2026 review): the human record is limited to phase I and small mechanistic studies; there are no controlled efficacy trials or long-term safety data.15

Regulatory status

GHRP-6 is not an FDA-approved drug, and DailyMed returned no label for it when searched on October 24, 2026.16 Since September 29, 2023, FDA has placed it in category 2 for outsourcing facilities (section 503B): bulk substances that may present significant safety risks.17 FDA cites a possible immune-reaction risk for certain routes, a potential effect on cortisol and higher blood sugar from reduced insulin sensitivity.17 For pharmacy compounding (section 503A), FDA's May 14, 2026 list puts GHRP-6 in category 3, substances nominated without adequate support.18 WADA bans it in and out of competition (class S2.2.4), and ClinicalTrials.gov lists no registered study.1920 Every vial is labeled: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.

Handling and storage

The vial holds freeze-dried powder of unstated salt form, and PubChem records GHRP-6 both as a free base and as an acetate.2 Independent stability data for GHRP-6 vials could not be found. FDA links the immune-reaction risk of compounded GHRP-6 to potential aggregation and peptide-related impurities.17 For lab work, see the reconstitution guide, and use the reconstitution calculator for the concentration math.

How should GHRP-6 be stored when it arrives?

What strengths of GHRP-6 are available?

The vial on this page holds 5 mg. Two related ghrelin-receptor peptides are listed separately: GHRP-2, from the same GHRP family, and Ipamorelin. In pigs, GHRP-6 raised ACTH and cortisol, while ipamorelin did not raise them beyond what GHRH did, even at more than 200 times its effective dose.21
