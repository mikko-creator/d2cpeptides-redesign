About Selank

Selank is a heptapeptide, a chain of 7 amino acids, designed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics of the Russian Academy of Sciences with the V.V. Zakusov Research Institute of Pharmacology.5 It extends tuftsin (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg), a fragment of the human immunoglobulin G heavy chain, with Pro-Gly-Pro to make the molecule more stable.5 Russia registers it as 0.15% nasal drops for adults with anxiety states.6 Here it is listed as a 5 mg research vial in the sleep and stress peptides range.

The human evidence is a handful of small Russian studies whose English abstracts omit doses, blinding and randomization.91011 A ClinicalTrials.gov search in October 2026 found no Selank trial.25 Read What is Selank? for the mechanism research, side effects and full source list.

Research highlights

  • Anxiety, 62 adults (2008): Selank and the benzodiazepine medazepam reduced anxiety to a similar degree, and Selank also showed anti-fatigue and stimulant effects.9
  • Anxiety, 60 adults (2014): against phenazepam, Selank showed a pronounced anti-anxiety effect that lasted a week after the last dose.10
  • Add-on use, 70 adults (2015): adding Selank to phenazepam brought an earlier response and fewer phenazepam side effects.11
  • Healthy volunteers (2020): in 52 adults split among Selank, Semax and placebo, brain scans 5 and 20 minutes after one injection showed altered links between the right amygdala and the right temporal cortex.12
  • Human plasma, lab study (2001): Selank inhibited enkephalin-degrading enzymes (enkephalins are natural opioid peptides), with 50% inhibition at about 15 micromolar.13
  • Rats (2022): one injection lowered a morphine-withdrawal score by 39.6%, close to the 49.3% seen with diazepam.14
  • Rats (2010): a single injection during training kept the memory of a learned task more stable over 30 days.15

Regulatory status

Selank is not FDA-approved.8 FDA's list of compounding substances that may present significant safety risks shows selank acetate as a withdrawn nomination, warns of possible immune reactions from aggregation and impurities, and says FDA lacks important human safety information.16 A 2020 FDA warning letter said drugs a pharmacy compounded from Selank did not qualify for the compounding exemptions.17 Selank was not on the agenda when FDA's compounding advisory committee reviewed peptides in July 2026.18 WADA's 2026 Prohibited List does not name Selank, and reviews disagree on its status: a 2025 review rated it unclear, while a 2026 review says WADA prohibits it.20218 The List's class S0 bans substances not covered elsewhere that have no current government approval for human therapeutic use, and Russia has approved Selank.206

Handling and storage

No label or published study found gives mixing steps for Selank powder.67 The Russian medicine needs none: it is a ready-made solution of 1.5 mg selank diacetate per mL with methylparaben as a preservative.76 For freeze-dried peptides in general, Bachem advises against storing them in solution and suggests frozen aliquots when a solution must be kept.22 The reconstitution guide sets out the lab steps, and how to store peptides covers freezer and fridge storage.

How should Selank be stored when it arrives?

Peptide makers ship freeze-dried peptides at room temperature and recommend a freezer for long-term storage, with a refrigerator at 4 °C acceptable for short-term use.22 The supplier's own figures give the Selank vial 36 months at -20 °C and 30 days at 2 to 8 °C; these are not published stability data.4 Once dissolved, peptides keep far less well than as powder.23

Is there a certificate of analysis for Selank?

The lab results page explains what testing documentation the store provides. When you read a certificate, look past the purity figure. Bachem notes that a peptide powder above 95% pure by HPLC may still be only 70 to 90% peptide by weight, because counter-ions such as acetate and residual water make up the rest.24 The supplier does not state which salt form of Selank its vial holds.4

What supplies are used with a Selank vial?

Is Selank for human use?

No. Every vial is labeled: For research use only. Not for human or veterinary use.
