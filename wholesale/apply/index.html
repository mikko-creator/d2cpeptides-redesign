Apply for a wholesale account

Tell us about your lab or business and what you plan to order. Wholesale pricing, any minimum order and lead times are confirmed when we review your application.

Before you apply

Have these details ready before you apply:

  • your company or lab name, your buyer type (research laboratory, university or research institute, reseller or online store, distributor, or other), your country and, if you have one, your website;
  • the product categories you need, and the products and strengths if you already know them;
  • an estimate of monthly volume, such as the number of vials a month; and
  • a contact name and email address, with an optional phone number and message.

The more specific you are about products, strengths and volume, the more specific our reply can be. Retail prices for every strength are on the product pages in the peptide shop and on the supplies page.

You also confirm that the products are for laboratory research use only. Wholesale buyers accept the same research use policy as every other customer, and the details you give help us confirm that an account fits it.

After you apply

We review each application and reply by email from support@d2cpeptides.com. We may ask for more details, such as which institution you work with or what research the order will support.

Pricing, any minimum order, volume tiers and lead times are confirmed on application, for the products and quantities you list. We ship to addresses across the United States, so if the order would go anywhere else, say where in your message.

For how accounts, orders and certificates of analysis work, read the wholesale page before you apply.
