Private and white label peptides

Private label and white label peptides for resellers that want research vials sold under their own brand. Which products we can supply under your label, and on what terms, is confirmed on application through our wholesale program.

Apply for a wholesale accountWholesale and bulk

Who private label is for

Private label and white label peptides suit resellers that already sell to research buyers under their own name: online stores, distributors and lab supply businesses. Instead of listing our brand next to theirs, they sell research vials under one brand, with their own labels and their own customer relationships.

Private label sits inside our wholesale program. The same account, the same research use terms and the same catalog apply, with your brand on the vial.

What we can and cannot offer

We are a distributor of finished research vials, not a manufacturer, and that sets the limits of any private label order:

  • Products: compounds, blends and strengths already in our catalog. We do not synthesize new sequences or mix custom blends.
  • Labels: your brand on the vial, with a research use statement that stays on every label.
  • Documents: a certificate of analysis stays tied to its lot, whatever the label says. Certificates are listed by product and lot on the lab results page when they are published, so your records can follow the lot through the rebrand.
  • Terms: minimums, lead times, label artwork and pricing are not published. They are confirmed on application.

How a private label request works

  1. Apply. Use the wholesale application and say in the message that you want private or white label supply. List the products and strengths, the quantities, and the market you sell to.
  2. Describe the label. Tell us what you want on the vial and on any outer packaging. Labels must carry a research use statement and must not suggest use in people.
  3. Confirm terms. We reply with what we can supply under your label, how labeling is handled, and the minimums and lead times for your order.
  4. Keep lot records. Record the lot number of every vial you rebrand, so any document or question can be traced back to it.

What changes when your brand is on the vial

When the label carries your brand, the labeling, the listings and the posts behind the product are yours. Federal rules judge a product's intended use by the objective intent of the people legally responsible for its labeling.2 That intent can be shown through labeling claims, advertising, written or spoken statements, the design of the product and the way it is distributed.2

A new name does not change what a product is. In August 2026 warning letters, sellers that had renamed GLP-1 drugs with codes were still treated by FDA as selling unapproved new drugs.34

The research use rules travel with the vial. To be shipped for lab research under the federal rule, a new drug must carry a caution that ends "Not for use in humans."1 The shipper must use due diligence to make sure the buyer is regularly engaged in such research, and FDA may end the authorization to ship if the drug is used for anything other than bona fide scientific investigation.1 If you ship our vials on to your own buyers under your brand, ask your counsel how those duties apply to you. You also agree to our research use policy when you apply.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between white label and private label peptides?

White label peptides are a supplier's standard product with the reseller's brand on the label: the same compound, strength and vial, sold under another name. Private label usually goes further, with packaging or a product range built to the reseller's own requirements. Because we resell finished vials rather than make them, any offer from us starts from the products and strengths in our catalog.

Is there a minimum order for private label peptides?

Minimums, lead times and label requirements are not published. They are confirmed on application. Start with the wholesale application. Describe the products, strengths and volumes you have in mind and how you plan to sell them, and we reply with what we can supply and on what terms.

Can you make a custom peptide or blend for my brand?

No. We do not synthesize peptides or mix custom blends. We resell finished research vials, so a private label order can only draw on compounds, blends and strengths already in our catalog. If your brand needs a new sequence or a blend we do not list, a contract synthesis lab is the right partner, and its documents would come from that lab.

Do private label vials keep a research use statement?

Yes. Every vial we supply is sold for laboratory research only, and a research use statement stays on it whatever brand it carries. For a new drug to be shipped for test-tube or lab animal research under the federal rule, its label must carry a set caution: that it is for investigational use only in laboratory research animals or for tests in vitro, and "Not for use in humans."1 The exact wording is confirmed on application.
