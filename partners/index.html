Partner program

A partner program for researchers, educators and publishers whose audiences buy research materials. It is not open yet: commission, payouts and tracking are not set, and they will be published here before any partnership starts. This page sets out who can apply, how the program is planned to work and the content rules every partner will follow.

Refer a friendContact us

Who the program is for

The partner program is for people and organizations whose audience buys research materials. That includes researchers and lab managers, educators who teach peptide chemistry or lab methods, publishers of research news and product reviews, and creators who cover peptide research.

It is built for ongoing referral relationships. A customer who simply wants to recommend us to a colleague can use refer a friend instead.

How the program will work

The plan is for each approved partner to get a landing page of its own with a tracking link, so orders from its audience are credited to it, and for commission to be paid on qualifying orders from the buyers a partner sends. Neither the landing pages nor the tracking exist yet.

None of the numbers are set yet. The commission rate, how long a referral counts, the payout method and schedule, and any minimum payout will be published on this page before any partnership starts. Orders that a partner sends are subject to our research use policy like any other order.

Content rules for partners

Every partner agrees to these rules for any content that mentions D2C Peptides or carries a partner link:

  1. Present our products as research materials, sold for laboratory research use only.
  2. Never give a dose, a frequency, a cycle, a "protocol" or injection steps for a person.
  3. Never say or suggest that a product treats, cures or prevents anything, and do not share personal-use stories, before-and-after images or results.
  4. Do not compare our research vials with prescription medicines, or present them as a substitute or a cheaper option.
  5. Do not aim content at anyone under 21.
  6. Say clearly, in every post or page that carries your link, that you are a D2C Peptides partner and may earn from referrals.

These rules exist because of how regulators read marketing. FDA has warned companies that sold research-labeled GLP-1 products directly to consumers for human use with dosing instructions.1 In its warning letters to peptide sellers, FDA has also pointed to social media. One letter quoted a Facebook post promoting cagrilintide with semaglutide for weight loss.2 Another noted the seller's Facebook, Instagram and YouTube pages, which sent consumers to its website.3

We will review partner content, and we may end a partnership that breaks these rules.

How to apply

To register your interest before the program opens, write to us at support@d2cpeptides.com, or use the contact page, with:

  • your name or organization, and where you publish;
  • who your audience is, and roughly how large it is;
  • two or three examples of your work on peptide research; and
  • how you would present our products.

We review each application against the content rules above. No partnership starts until the terms are set and published on this page. Before you apply, the peptide shop shows the full catalog your audience would see, with every strength and its stock status.

Frequently asked questions

How much commission do partners earn?

The commission rate has not been set, so this page does not promise one. The rate, how long a referral counts, the payout method, the payout schedule and any minimum payout will be published here once they are set, before any partnership starts. Until then, you can register your interest with the details listed under how to apply.

Who can join the partner program?

People and organizations whose audience buys materials for laboratory research: researchers and lab managers, educators who teach peptide chemistry or lab methods, publishers of research news, and creators who cover peptide research. Your content must not be aimed at anyone under 21, and you must be willing to present our products as research materials only. We decline applications built around personal use, dosing or results.

Can I post dosing or results content with my partner link?

No. A partner link must never sit next to a dose, a schedule, injection steps or a personal result. FDA has warned companies that sold research-labeled GLP-1 products directly to consumers for human use with dosing instructions,1 and in its letters to peptide sellers it has quoted social media posts as evidence of intended human use.23 Content like that puts you, your audience and us at risk.

How is the partner program different from refer a friend?

The partner program is for people who send us an ongoing audience, with commission terms to be published once they are set. Refer a friend is for customers who want to recommend us to a colleague, with any referral credit listed on that page once confirmed. If you are unsure which fits, write to us and describe how you would share D2C Peptides.
