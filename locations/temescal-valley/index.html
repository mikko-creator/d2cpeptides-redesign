Keeping peptides cool in Temescal Valley

No NOAA normals station sits inside the community. The nearest are Elsinore, about 9 miles southeast at 1,268 feet, and Riverside Municipal Airport, about 13.5 miles north at 805 feet.563 The ground at the community's center point is about 1,146 feet up.73

The two stations frame the local heat. Elsinore's June to August normal high is 94.8 °F, with about 123 days a year at 90 °F or more and about 42 at 100 °F or more.5 Riverside Municipal Airport's is 91.6 °F, with about 100 days at 90 °F or more and about 22 at 100 °F or more.6 Precipitation runs 11.65 and 9.37 inches a year respectively.56

Peptides keep far less well once dissolved. GenScript says their shelf life in solution is very limited, much shorter than in freeze-dried form, and that peptides stored in solution are susceptible to bacterial degradation.9 Bachem advises against storing peptides in solution at all, even sterile and oxygen-free solution, because they may slowly break down chemically. Frozen solutions, it says, may be kept for a few weeks.8

If solution storage cannot be avoided, GenScript's advice is to dissolve the peptide in a buffer at pH 5 to 6, split it into aliquots, store them at -20 °C and avoid freeze-thaw cycles. A 0.2 micron filter can remove possible bacterial contamination.9 The labels for Bacteriostatic Water and Sterile Water for Injection both say to mix thoroughly, use promptly and not store reconstituted solutions unless the maker of the dissolved drug directs otherwise.1011 The reconstitution guide and the storage guide cover the rest.

About Temescal Valley

The Census Bureau counts Temescal Valley as a census-designated place: a statistical area, not an incorporated city. It covers 19.336 square miles of land.3 About 99.5% of that land lies in Census ZIP Code Tabulation Area 92883, with a sliver in 92530, and 92883 also takes in part of the City of Corona.4 The 2020 Census counted 26,232 residents. The 2020 to 2024 American Community Survey estimate is 30,069, with a margin of error of 1,987.121

Without a city council, residents advise Riverside County through the Temescal Valley Municipal Advisory Council. It was set up in 1998 as the Temescal Canyon council and later renamed. Its territory follows Interstate 15 from roughly Corona in the north to Lake Elsinore in the south, with the Cleveland National Forest to the west, inside the county's Second Supervisorial District.2

The county's Temescal Canyon Area Plan describes the corridor as a mix of suburban and rural estate neighborhoods, a considerable amount of industrial uses and extensive areas of existing and potential mineral extraction.13 The same plan calls Glen Ivy Hot Springs, a day spa with natural hot springs and mud baths, a popular tourist destination along Interstate 15.13

Housing is newer and mostly owned. 86.7% of occupied homes are owner-occupied. 46.1% of housing units were built from 2000 to 2009 and 7.3% in 2020 or later, against 2.0% countywide.1415 The median age is 40.1.1 Workers average 42.6 minutes to get to work, against 33.8 across the county, and 44.7% hold management, business, science and arts jobs, against 33.6%.1617
