The storage target comes from peptide makers, not the weather. Bachem, a peptide maker, ships freeze-dried peptides at room temperature but advises a refrigerator at 4 °C for short-term use and a freezer below -15 °C, in a tightly closed container, for longer storage.9 Bring a delivered box indoors promptly and store each vial as its label or product page directs. About Anthem

Del Webb founded Anthem in 1999, and the community was initially built out in 2008.1 Three homeowners' associations manage its residential neighborhoods: Parkside, Country Club and The Village.1 It lies about 17 miles from Phoenix, measured south to the Loop 101, and about 120 miles from Flagstaff by Interstate 17.1 The 2020 Census counted 23,190 residents and 9,278 housing units in the Anthem census-designated place, which covers 7.9 square miles of land.108 The Anthem Community Council puts today's population at more than 27,000, a community figure rather than a Census count.1

Census survey data describe an older, owner-occupied community. The median age is 46.3, against 37.6 for Maricopa County, and about 84.9 percent of occupied homes are owned, against about 65.2 percent countywide (American Community Survey, 2020 to 2024).1112 The council lists 15 miles of trails, 1,400 acres of protected washes and open space, and more than 20,000 trees.1 In Community Park, the Anthem Veterans Memorial has five pillars for the five branches of the U.S. military. At 11:11 a.m. each Veterans Day, sunlight passes through the pillars to light a glass mosaic of the Great Seal of the United States.13

Fire and emergency medical service comes from the Daisy Mountain Fire & Medical District, formed in 1989, which also covers Black Canyon City, Cave Creek, Desert Hills and New River.14 A nearby hospital is HonorHealth Sonoran Crossing Medical Center in north Phoenix, a 79-bed acute care hospital that opened in fall 2020.15

Frequently asked questions

Anthem is unincorporated. Can orders still be delivered there?

Yes. Anthem is a master-planned community, not a city, and its council notes that the Parkside area west of Interstate 17 is served by the City of Phoenix while the east side falls under Maricopa County.1 None of that affects delivery, which depends only on the street address. Orders ship from the USA and Canada under the shipping policy used across the United States, and the returns page covers a damaged parcel.

What does TRZ GLP2 mean on the tirzepatide listing?

TRZ GLP2 is the store name under which our tirzepatide research vial is sold. The vial comes in 5 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg, and the product page shows the price and stock for each strength. It is one of three GLP-1 research vials in the catalog and is sold for laboratory research only. For what published studies report about the compound, see What is Tirzepatide?.

Is Anthem cooler than Phoenix for storing peptides?

Somewhat, but not enough to change how a parcel should be handled. Anthem sits at about 1,790 feet,3 and the two NOAA stations used for it, Phoenix Deer Valley Airport to the south and Carefree to the east, average 90.0 and 81.4 days a year at or above 100 °F.24 Phoenix Sky Harbor averages 111.3.5 With July highs still above 103 °F at both stations, a delivered box should come indoors promptly.67

Is there a D2C Peptides location in Anthem?

No. Anthem's council counts nearly 300 storefront businesses,1 but D2C Peptides lists no location in Anthem or anywhere else in Maricopa County, so this page has no address, phone number or map. Orders reach Anthem by shipping only. The contact page handles questions, and Peptides in Phoenix links the county's other pages.
