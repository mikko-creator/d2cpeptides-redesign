Keeping peptides cool in Menifee

The nearest NOAA normals station is Elsinore, at 1,268 feet, about 9.3 miles west of Menifee's center point.478 August there averages a high of 97.8 °F and a low of 64.8 °F, and December a high of 65.0 °F and a low of 40.3 °F.4 The station logs 42.3 days a year at 100 °F or more and 123.0 days at 90 °F or more, with 9.0 freezing nights and 11.65 inches of precipitation.2 The San Jacinto station, about 14.1 miles east-northeast, averages 39.4 days at 100 °F or more and 14.9 freezing nights.97

Commutes are long here: the average trip to work takes 41.5 minutes, and 14.2% of workers work from home.10 On a workday, a delivered parcel may sit outside for hours before anyone is home.

Bachem, a peptide maker, typically ships its peptides at room temperature and prints the recommended long-term storage condition on the label.5 Dry powder is the sturdier form: GenScript, another maker, notes that peptides in solution have a far shorter shelf life than freeze-dried ones.11 Keep vials dry and unmixed until they are needed, and use the storage guide for temperatures once the parcel is inside.

About Menifee

Menifee had 118,592 residents on July 1, 2025, fourth among the county's 28 incorporated cities, up from an April 2020 base of 102,526.1 It covers 46.462 square miles of land and lies along Interstate 215 in the county's southwest.73 Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas 92584, 92585, 92586 and 92587 cover the city, and 92584 alone holds about half of its land.6 Voters approved incorporation on June 3, 2008, and Menifee became the county's 26th city on October 1, 2008. It is named for Luther Menifee Wilson, a miner who found a significant quartz lode in the area in the early 1880s.3

The valley was first called Paloma, or dove, Valley, and "Dove Valley", also known as Quail Valley, was a popular hunting spot in the early 1900s.3 Sun City, in the center of the city, began in the early 1960s as an active retirement community planned by Del Webb, the Phoenix builder who also developed Sun City, Arizona. Menifee Lakes grew as a master-planned community in the late 1980s and early 1990s.3 Today 17.4% of residents are 65 or older, and the median age is 38.1.12

Menifee Global Medical Center, in the Sun City area, is a general acute care hospital with a basic emergency department and 84 licensed beds.13 Mt. San Jacinto College opened its Menifee Valley Campus in 1990 as the district's second campus, and it houses the college's Allied Health programs.14
