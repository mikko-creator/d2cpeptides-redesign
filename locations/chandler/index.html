Keeping peptides cool in Chandler

Two NOAA stations with 1991 to 2020 normals bracket Chandler. Tempe ASU, about 1,167 feet above sea level, lies some 10.7 miles north-northwest of the city's Census center point. Chandler Heights, at about 1,425 feet, lies some 11.2 miles east-southeast.91011 The city's own center point sits at about 1,207 feet.12

The Chandler Heights figures rest on about 12 to 15 years of records, fewer than a full 30-year normal.11 Between them, the two stations average about 95 to 107 days a year at or above 100 °F and around ten nights at or below freezing.12

For dry, unopened vials the main job is getting them out of the heat and into the storage named on the label or product page. Once a vial is mixed, the rules tighten. Bachem, a peptide maker, says peptides should not be stored in solution, even sterile and oxygen-free solution, because they may slowly break down chemically; frozen solutions may keep for a few weeks.5 GenScript adds that solutions can also be degraded by bacteria.6 Where solution storage cannot be avoided, GenScript advises dissolving the peptide in a buffer at pH 5 to 6, splitting it into aliquots and keeping them at -20 °C.6 The storage guide and the reconstitution guide go into more detail.

About Chandler

Dr. A.J. Chandler founded the city in 1912, and Arizona's original golf resort opened there a year later.13 The Census Bureau estimates 278,748 residents on July 1, 2025, a little below the 2022 peak of 280,356, on 65.7 square miles of land.149 The city sits in the southeast part of greater Phoenix, served by Interstate 10, the Loop 202 San Tan Freeway, the Loop 101 Price Freeway and U.S. 60.13 It counts 70 parks, about one for every square mile, including Tumbleweed Park and Veterans Oasis Park, plus the Chandler Museum and the Arizona Railway Museum.13

Technology leads the city's employer list. Intel is its largest private employer, with 10,000 Chandler-based jobs, ahead of Wells Fargo (5,500), Bank of America (3,600) and Dignity Health's Chandler Regional Medical Center (3,000).15 Intel has completed Fab 52, the fifth high-volume fab at its Ocotillo Campus.3 The city also lists an ultra-pure nitrogen pipeline and a citywide fiber network among its business infrastructure.13

Hospitals and campuses add to the mix. Banner Health opened a four-story, 240,000 square foot medical center in Chandler in 2020, with 124 beds.3 Downtown hosts the ASU Chandler Innovation Center and a University of Arizona regional learning center, and Chandler-Gilbert Community College is in the city.13 Census survey data for 2024 put the median age at 38.9 and the share of adults 25 and older with a bachelor's degree or higher at 50.7 percent.161718

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver to every part of Chandler?

Yes. Every Chandler address is served the same way, whether it is in West Chandler, the Price Corridor or the Airpark Area, three areas the city names in its business news.3 Parcels ship from the USA and Canada under the shipping policy for every order across the United States, and each product page shows strengths and stock before checkout. If an item arrives damaged or missing, the returns page lists what to send us.

Which supplies does the HCG product page list?

HCG is sold as a 5000 IU vial, and its product page lists these lab supplies alongside it: bacteriostatic water, syringes, an 18G needle and alcohol swabs. The reconstitution calculator turns the vial's contents and the volume of diluent into a concentration. Hospira's label for Bacteriostatic Water for Injection, USP describes a multiple-dose vial with benzyl alcohol as the preservative, and says to use aseptic technique for every entry into the container.4

How long does a vial keep once it has been mixed?

There is no single answer. Peptide makers say peptides in solution have a very limited shelf life compared with the freeze-dried powder, and they advise against storing them that way.56 Approved products show how much the limits vary: one freeze-dried tesamorelin product must be used right away, while a chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) product may be refrigerated after mixing and must be discarded after 60 days.78 Even the two tesamorelin formulations differ in reconstitution and storage requirements, and their label says they are not substitutable.19
