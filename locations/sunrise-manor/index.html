Keeping peptides cool in Sunrise Manor

The valley's main long-record weather station, at the airport, lies about 9.8 miles southwest of Sunrise Manor's internal point.53 NOAA's 1991 to 2020 averages there give July highs of 104.5 °F and January lows of 40.5 °F, with 137.1 days a year at or above 90 °F.56 Nights on this side of the valley can run cooler. The National Weather Service says the valley's lowest temperatures are often recorded on its eastern side, which sits lower and where colder air often drains at night.4 The ground at Sunrise Manor's internal point is about 1,759 feet above sea level.2 Days are still extreme across the valley: the official record high, measured at the airport, is 120 °F, from July 7, 2024.13

Whatever the night brings, the bigger storage difference is between powder and liquid. Peptide makers treat the freeze-dried powder as the stable form. Bachem, a peptide manufacturer, says peptides should not be stored in solution, even sterile, oxygen-free solution, because they may slowly break down chemically, although frozen solutions may be kept for a few weeks.14 GenScript says a peptide's shelf life in solution is very limited and much shorter than in freeze-dried form, especially for sequences containing Cys, Met, Trp, Asp, Gln or N-terminal Glu, and that bacteria can also degrade a stored solution.15

The order of operations follows from that. Keep vials dry and cold until they are needed, and mix only what the work calls for. The reconstitution guide and the storage guide explain both steps.

About Sunrise Manor

Sunrise Manor has no city hall of its own: it is one of Clark County's unincorporated towns.1 Its population depends on the boundary used: the 2020 Census counted 205,618 residents in the Census Bureau's Sunrise Manor area16 but 209,310 in the town as the state reports it.1 Nevada's certified estimate for the town is 212,012 on July 1, 2025, and the county's August 2025 figure is 213,471.117 The Census area spans 33.713 square miles, all of it land.3 To the east, Sunrise Mountain and Frenchman Mountain separate the valley from Lake Mead.4

It is a young community: the median age is 33.7 years, 27.3 percent of residents are under 18 and 12.0 percent are 65 or older, and households average 3.11 people (ACS 2020 to 2024).1819 About 55.8 percent of residents are Hispanic or Latino.18 Half of residents age 5 and older (50.3 percent) speak a language other than English at home, and 44.9 percent speak Spanish.19 Just over half of occupied homes (51.7 percent) are owner-occupied,20 and the mean commute is 28.3 minutes.21

None of the 26 Clark County hospitals in CMS's Hospital General Information list has an address in the town's four main ZIP codes, 89110, 89115, 89142 and 89156.228 Nellis Air Force Base lies just to the north: the Census Bureau's internal point for the Nellis AFB area sits about 4.7 miles north of Sunrise Manor's.3 The Air Force puts the base about eight miles northeast of Las Vegas and its workforce at about 14,000 military and civilians, one of the largest single employers in Southern Nevada; counting family members and retirees, the area's military population is close to 60,000.23 The base's 99th Medical Group provides medical services at the Mike O'Callaghan Military Medical Center.23

Frequently asked questions

Is Sunrise Manor a city?

No. Sunrise Manor is an unincorporated town of Clark County, with a Town Advisory Board that meets at the Hollywood Community Services Center instead of a city council.17 It is large for a town: Nevada's certified estimate puts it at 212,012 residents on July 1, 2025.1 For ordering, its status makes no difference: every Clark County address is handled alike.

Which ZIP codes cover Sunrise Manor?

Measured by the Census Bureau's ZIP code areas, which approximate USPS ZIP codes, four codes cover about 82 percent of Sunrise Manor's land as the Census Bureau maps it: 89110, 89115, 89142 and 89156. Only 89142 lies entirely inside it. Parts of 89104, 89121 and 89122 also fall within the boundary, eight areas in all.8 Orders to any of these codes ship from the USA and Canada under the shipping policy.

Does storage advice change on the valley's cooler east side?

No. Storage limits come from each product's label and from how it is kept indoors, not from the night air outside. Once a peptide is mixed, those limits spread widely: among FDA-approved freeze-dried products, EGRIFTA SV is used immediately, EGRIFTA WR keeps at 20 to 25 °C for up to 7 days, ZOMACTON is refrigerated for up to 14 days (5 mg vial) or 28 days (10 mg vial), and a Fresenius Kabi hCG vial may be refrigerated but is discarded after 60 days.9101112 Bachem and GenScript, two peptide makers, advise against storing research peptides in solution.1415
