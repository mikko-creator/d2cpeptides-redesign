Keeping peptides cool in Winchester

The valley's airport weather station lies about 4.7 miles south-southwest of Winchester's internal point.32 Over 1991 to 2020, that station's normals put June to August highs at 102.2 °F and July lows at 82.0 °F, so summer nights stay hot.83 January runs from a normal low of 40.5 °F to a high of 58.5 °F, and the station averages 78.2 days a year at or above 100 °F with 4.18 inches of precipitation.38 The National Weather Service's official record, kept since January 1, 1937, runs from a low of 8 °F, most recently on January 13, 1963, to a high of 120 °F on July 7, 2024.9

Once a parcel is inside, the handling questions shift from heat to the open vial, where CDC's 28-day limit for an opened multiple-dose vial, unless its maker states otherwise, is the starting point.5 Hospira's label for Bacteriostatic Water for Injection calls for aseptic technique at every entry into and withdrawal from the container.10 Sterile Water for Injection, which has no preservative, comes only in single-dose containers, and CDC says leftover single-dose contents should not be pooled or stored for later use.115 Both water labels say a reconstituted drug should be mixed thoroughly, used promptly and not stored, unless the maker of the dissolved drug directs otherwise.1011 Step-by-step versions sit in the storage guide and the guide to reconstituting a vial.

About Winchester

Winchester is one of Clark County's unincorporated towns, not a city.1 Its Town Advisory Board meets at the Winchester Community Center, and the county lists two commissioners with the board, for Districts E and F.12 Its land area is just 4.96 square miles, with no water, far smaller than Paradise (42.40 square miles), Enterprise (66.00) or North Las Vegas (105.35).2 Nevada's certified estimate puts the population at 33,894 on July 1, 2025, and the county's August 2025 estimate at 34,127.17 Census survey figures for the census-designated place run higher, 37,081 for 2020 to 2024, which works out to about 7,479 people per square mile.132

The Winchester Dondero Cultural Center, run by Clark County Parks and Recreation, was dedicated on August 4, 1982 as a community center and became the county's only cultural center nine years later.14 It sits on an 8.9-acre parcel and holds Clark County's only indoor theater, with 220 seats.14

Most households rent: 68.0 percent of occupied homes are renter-occupied.15 More than half of residents (52.3 percent) are Hispanic or Latino, 35.6 percent were born outside the United States, and 41.2 percent of residents 5 and over speak Spanish at home.1316 About one in four employed residents (25.6 percent) works in arts, entertainment, recreation, accommodation and food services, and 15.3 percent in retail.17 The median age is 35.7 years, with 21.4 percent of residents under 18 and 11.5 percent 65 or older.13

Frequently asked questions

Where is Winchester, Nevada?

Winchester is a small unincorporated town of Clark County.1 Its internal point, the reference spot the Census Bureau places inside each area, lies about 3.4 miles north of Paradise's, and the town covers 4.96 square miles of land, far less than Paradise (42.40) or Enterprise (66.00).2 No ZIP code area lies wholly inside it; by land, about 39 percent is in 89109, 29 percent in 89121 and 18 percent in 89169, with smaller parts in 89102 and 89104.4 Orders to all of them ship from the USA and Canada.

If I order bacteriostatic water to Winchester, how long does an opened vial last?

CDC injection safety guidance says a multiple-dose vial, once opened or punctured by a needle, should be dated and discarded within 28 days unless the manufacturer states a different date, and never used past the manufacturer's expiration date.5 The EGRIFTA WR instructions likewise say to discard leftover Bacteriostatic Water for Injection 28 days after first use.6 For the bacteriostatic water sold here, check the date on its own label.

Is Winchester growing like the rest of the valley?

No, it has held almost steady. Nevada's certified estimates put Winchester at 34,095 residents in July 2019 and 33,894 in July 2025. Over the same six years Clark County grew from 2,293,391 to 2,448,576, and Enterprise, now Nevada's largest unincorporated town, grew from 211,761 to 259,959.1 Apartments house about 45 percent of Winchester's residents.7
