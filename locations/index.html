Locations

D2C Peptides has place pages in three areas: greater Phoenix in Maricopa County, Arizona; the Las Vegas Valley in Clark County, Nevada; and Riverside County, California. Orders reach all of them by shipping from the USA and Canada, and each page adds local notes on keeping a parcel cool in the heat.

How the location pages work

Each place page follows the same plan. It explains how orders reach the place, always by shipping, and names the products most searched with the place's name. It gives climate figures from a nearby NOAA weather station and adds sourced facts about the place itself. No page lists a street address, because D2C Peptides has no staffed location in any of the three counties. The shipping page holds the policy that every order follows.

Maricopa County, Arizona

Maricopa County is the largest of the three, with 4,689,558 residents on July 1, 2025, more than half of all Arizonans.12 It covers 9,224 square miles and includes 24 cities and towns, with Phoenix, home to 1,665,481 people, as its seat.21 Two of our pages there, Surprise in the northwest Valley and Queen Creek in the southeast, sit about 57 miles apart in a straight line between their Census center points.3 The hub is Peptides in Phoenix. The weather station at Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport averages 111.3 days a year at or above 100 °F.4

Clark County, Nevada

Clark County had 2,407,226 residents on July 1, 2025, about 73 percent of Nevada's people, on 7,891.7 square miles of land.56 Nearly all of them live in one metro area: the county's own estimate places 2,396,409 of its 2,465,431 residents in the Las Vegas Valley urban area.7 Four of our seven pages there cover unincorporated areas rather than cities: Paradise, Sunrise Manor, Winchester and Enterprise.7 The hub is Peptides in Las Vegas. The Las Vegas airport weather station averages 78.2 days a year at or above 100 °F.8

Riverside County, California

Riverside County had 2,544,916 residents on July 1, 2025, the fourth-largest population of California's 58 counties, on 7,209 square miles of land.910 It has 28 incorporated cities, and about 17 percent of its residents live outside any of them.9 Its climate differs sharply between the western valleys and the low desert. Riverside Municipal Airport averages about 22 days a year at or above 100 °F, while Palm Springs averages about 123 such days.1112 Our pages run from western cities such as Corona and Temecula to Palm Springs and La Quinta, which lie in the supervisorial district that covers the eastern two-thirds of the county.13 The hub is Peptides in Riverside.

Heat and storage in every area

All three counties have long, hot summers, and each place page gives its local numbers. The handling rule is the same everywhere. Bachem, a peptide maker, says its products are typically shipped at room temperature, with the recommended long-term storage condition noted on the label.14 The care starts at delivery: bring the parcel inside promptly, then store each vial as its label or product page directs. The storage guide explains the details, and the lab results page lists lab documents by product and lot number as they become available.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas have location pages?

Three counties, each with a hub page. Maricopa County in Arizona starts at Peptides in Phoenix, Clark County in Nevada at Peptides in Las Vegas, and Riverside County in California at Peptides in Riverside. Together they hold 34 place pages (cities, towns and unincorporated communities), listed on this page under their county. Each one covers delivery, the products most searched with the place's name, the local climate and sourced facts about the place.

Can I collect an order at one of these places?

No. D2C Peptides has no staffed location in any of the three counties, so no place page lists a street address, phone number or map. Every order is delivered by shipping from the USA and Canada, as set out on the shipping page. Questions about an order go through the contact page.

Do you ship outside these three counties?

Yes. Orders ship across the United States, not only to the places listed here. These pages exist for people who search for peptides by place name, and because summer heat in all three counties makes storage on arrival worth a local note. The shipping page sets out the policy every order follows, wherever it goes, and the returns page covers items that arrive damaged, wrong or missing.

Is the same catalog available in every location?

Yes. Every place can order the full catalog of 45 peptides and 7 lab supplies, all sold for laboratory research only under the research use policy. A place page features the products most often searched together with that place's name, such as tirzepatide for Anthem or HCG for Mesa. Strengths, prices and stock for each ship-from origin are on the product pages in the peptide shop.
